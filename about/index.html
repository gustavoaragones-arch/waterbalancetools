About WaterBalanceTools

An independent water chemistry reference for pool and hot tub owners.

What This Site Is

WaterBalanceTools is a free reference library for pool and hot tub water chemistry. It provides calculators, printable checklists, chemistry guides, and reference tables — all in one place, with no paywalls and no email required.

The goal is to make pool chemistry understandable and actionable for everyday pool owners, not just certified technicians.

Who Runs It

WaterBalanceTools.com is owned and operated by Albor Digital LLC, an independent digital media company based in the United States.

Methodology

All chemical dosing calculations are based on published industry standards and widely accepted water chemistry formulas. For a detailed explanation of how each calculator works, see the Methodology page.

Accuracy & Disclaimer

While we strive for accuracy, results from these calculators are estimates. Always verify chemical levels with a reliable test kit before swimming. For commercial pools or complex water chemistry problems, consult a certified pool operator.
