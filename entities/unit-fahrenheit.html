Unit Entity

°F

Degrees Fahrenheit — the standard temperature unit for pool and spa temperature in North America.

Definition Quick Facts Relationships Calculators Related Entities

Definition

Fahrenheit is the US standard for temperature measurement. Pool temperature targets: 78–84°F (residential pools), 100–104°F maximum (hot tubs). LSI temperature factor tables use °F. Conversion: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.
