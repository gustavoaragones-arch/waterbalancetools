Definition

The Pool & Hot Tub Alliance (formerly APSP) is the primary trade association for pool and spa manufacturers, builders, service technicians, and retailers. PHTA publishes the Pool & Spa Operator Handbook (the industry standard reference for pool chemistry) and the ANSI standards for pool design, construction, and safety. PHTA certifications (CPO, ATP) are the recognized professional credentials in the pool industry.
