Chemical Entity

Combined Chlorine

Chlorine that has reacted with contaminants and is no longer effective for sanitization.

Definition Quick Facts Relationships Calculators Related Entities

Definition

Combined chlorine (CC) is the difference between total chlorine and free chlorine. It consists primarily of chloramines — compounds formed when chlorine reacts with ammonia, urea, and other nitrogen compounds from bathers. Chloramines are responsible for the "pool smell," eye irritation, and respiratory irritation often mistakenly blamed on too much chlorine. When CC exceeds 0.5 ppm, breakpoint chlorination (shock treatment) is required.
