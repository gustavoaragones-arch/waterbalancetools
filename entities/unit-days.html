Days

Definition Quick Facts Relationships Calculators Related Entities

Definition

Days are used for maintenance scheduling (test twice per week, shock weekly), CYA dissolution time (24–48 days), water change intervals for hot tubs (every 90 days), and chemical activity timelines.
