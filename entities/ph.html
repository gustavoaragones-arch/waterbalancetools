Related Entities

Measurement

Total Alkalinity

The capacity of water to resist pH changes — a pH buffer.
Measurement

Langelier Saturation Index

A composite score indicating whether water is balanced, corrosive, or scale-forming.
Chemical

Free Chlorine

The active, unsaturated chlorine available to sanitize pool water.
Chemical

Calcium Hardness

The concentration of dissolved calcium in pool water, affecting scaling and corrosion tendency.
Chemical Product

Muriatic Acid

Hydrochloric acid (31.45%) used to lower pH and total alkalinity in pool water.
Chemical Product

Soda Ash

Sodium carbonate — used to raise pH in pool water.
Problem

Scaling

White or grey mineral deposits on pool surfaces and equipment caused by over-saturated water.
Problem

Corrosion

Damage to pool surfaces and metal equipment caused by corrosive (low LSI) water.
