Taylor Technologies

Taylor Technologies — manufacturer of professional pool test kits and the standard reference for pool chemistry calculations.

Definition Quick Facts Relationships Calculators Related Entities

Definition

Taylor Technologies is the leading manufacturer of professional pool and spa water test kits (the Taylor K-2006 is the gold-standard residential test kit) and the publisher of the Pool/Spa Water Chemistry book, which is the definitive technical reference for pool chemistry calculations, LSI factors, and treatment formulas. Taylor LSI factor tables and formulas are used as the basis for LSI calculations throughout the industry.
