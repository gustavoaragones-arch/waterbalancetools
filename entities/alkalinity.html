Measurement Entity

Total Alkalinity

The capacity of water to resist pH changes — a pH buffer.

Definition Quick Facts Relationships Calculators Related Entities

Definition

Total alkalinity (TA) measures the concentration of alkaline compounds — primarily bicarbonates, carbonates, and hydroxides — dissolved in pool water. It acts as a pH buffer, resisting pH swings caused by additions of acid, base, or CO₂. Low TA causes pH to swing wildly with minor chemical additions (pH bounce). High TA makes pH very difficult to lower and increases scaling tendency. Total alkalinity must be adjusted before pH — it determines how much chemical is needed for a given pH change.
