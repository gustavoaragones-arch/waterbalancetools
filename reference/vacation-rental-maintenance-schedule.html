Vacation Rental Pool Maintenance Schedule

v2026.07

A complete printable maintenance schedule for managing pool or spa chemistry at a vacation rental property.

Overview

This schedule ensures consistent pool management at vacation rental properties regardless of guest frequency or occupancy level.

Task Frequency and Responsibility

TaskFrequencyWhoDocument in Log?
Test FC and pHAt every turnoverProperty manager / pool serviceYes
Full water test (all parameters)WeeklyPool serviceYes
Shock treatmentWeekly or after heavy usePool serviceYes
Skimmer basket checkAt every turnoverProperty managerNote if heavy debris
Brush pool walls and floorWeeklyPool serviceNote if algae
Filter pressure checkWeeklyPool serviceYes
Filter clean / backwashWhen pressure is 8–10 psi over baselinePool serviceYes
Safety equipment checkAt every turnoverProperty managerYes
Chemical storage checkMonthlyProperty ownerYes
CYA and hardness testMonthlyPool serviceYes
Full equipment inspectionMonthlyPool serviceYes
Water change (hot tub)Every 3–4 guest staysPool serviceYes

Notes

  • Log all tests and treatments in the pool chemical log.
  • Pre-turnover test clears the pool for incoming guests.
  • Close pool to guests if FC is below 1 ppm (pool) or 3 ppm (hot tub) until corrected.
