Pool Pump Sizing Guide

v2026.07

Pool pump sizing starts with pool volume and target turnover time. This guide shows required GPM for common pool sizes.

Overview

The correct pump flow rate (GPM) equals pool volume divided by target turnover time in minutes.

Notes

  • Always verify actual GPM at your system head pressure — pump ratings are at zero head.
  • Variable speed pumps should be selected for the highest required flow, then programmed at lower speeds for routine filtration.
