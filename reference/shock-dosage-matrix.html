Shock Dosage Matrix

v2026.07

Quick reference for how many pounds of calcium hypochlorite (65%) are needed to shock a pool by volume and purpose.

Overview

These doses are for 65% calcium hypochlorite (cal-hypo). Adjust proportionally for other concentrations. Always add pre-dissolved to the pool, never to the skimmer.

Cal-Hypo (65%) Shock Dose by Pool Volume

Notes

  • Doses use the site-wide approved mass-balance formula (ppm increase x gallons x 0.013344 / 65) for 65% calcium hypochlorite. A breakpoint dose is 10x the combined-chlorine (CC) reading, so a CC of 1 ppm requires the same 10 ppm FC raise as a routine maintenance dose -- the two leftmost dose columns are the same figure for that reason, not an error. Green pool dose targets a 30 ppm FC raise. These figures are a manual reference; the live shock calculator offers fixed presets (5, 10, 15, or 20 ppm) rather than the full range shown here.
  • Pre-dissolve granular shock in a bucket of water before adding to pool.
  • Add after dark. Run pump overnight. Do not allow swimming until FC drops below 5 ppm.
  • For 73% cal-hypo, multiply doses by 0.89.
