Closing Chemical Kit

A pool closing chemical kit contains the chemicals needed to balance and protect pool water during winter dormancy.

In Plain Language

Closing kits typically include: shock, algaecide (winter formula), stain inhibitor, and sometimes a floating chlorine dispenser. The goal is to put the pool to sleep with balanced water that will resist algae and scale during months of inactivity. Winterising requires correct pH and alkalinity balance, an algaecide dose, and enough chlorine to maintain a residual for several weeks.

Why It Matters

Proper chemical closing prevents severe algae blooms that would otherwise develop over winter, requiring costly spring recovery.
