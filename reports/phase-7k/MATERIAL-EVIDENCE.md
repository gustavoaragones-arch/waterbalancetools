# Phase 7K — Material-Science Claim Evidence

Material-property claims (fiberglass/vinyl/concrete pool surfaces) were evaluated against material-industry/manufacturer sources, not generic water-chemistry sources, per the Director's explicit instruction that chemistry sources should not be forced to stand in for material-science claims.

## Vinyl liner bleaching (`ec-vinyl-pool-0282`) — SUPPORTED

Source: Coated Fabrics and Film Association (CFFA), Vinyl Pool Liners technical division — a genuine material-industry trade association, not a pool-chemistry publisher. Directly confirms the site's claim: undissolved granular chemicals (trichlor named specifically) settling on a vinyl liner surface cause localized bleaching, citing exposure times as short as 6 hours, and explicitly naming "shock product hasn't been pre-dissolved" as a cause. New source: `cffa-vinyl-liner-bleaching`.

## Fiberglass gelcoat / calcium hardness (`ec-fiberglass-pool-0286`) — CONTEXTUAL

Source: Orenda Technologies, a pool-water-chemistry manufacturer with published technical material specifically addressing fiberglass. Confirms the underlying mechanism (aggressive, low-calcium water can chalk/etch a fiberglass gelcoat because fiberglass, unlike plaster, has no calcium of its own for the water to draw from) but explicitly pushes back on treating "keep calcium under 200 ppm" as a clean universal rule, recommending chelation-based management instead. The site's existing 150-250 ppm figure is a reasonable, defensible range but the topic carries real industry nuance a single number doesn't fully capture. No page rewrite: the core mechanism claim is not contradicted, only oversimplified — and oversimplification of a defensible range is not the kind of "verified factual correction" this phase's minimum-necessary-change rule is meant to trigger.

## Concrete/gunite pools — not separately researched this phase

No specific numeric or safety claim from `concrete-pool`'s entity content appeared in the P0-P2 priority queue requiring dedicated material-source research this phase; its claims were reviewed as part of the general 61-entity triage and found to be either non-numeric definitional statements or already covered by the general calcium-hardness range shared with fiberglass/vinyl.
