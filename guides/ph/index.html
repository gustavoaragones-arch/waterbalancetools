Can You Swim in High pH Pool Water?

Can you swim in high pH water? pH 7.8–8.0 is uncomfortable; above 8.0 risks irritation and drastically reduced chlorine. Full guidance inside.
Does Rain Lower Pool pH?

Does rain lower pool pH? Rainwater is acidic (pH ~5.6) but the effect on pool pH depends on pool volume, TA, and how much rain fell.

What Causes High Pool pH?

High pool pH causes: CO2 off-gassing, high alkalinity, algae, aeration, SWG systems, and high-pH fill water. Fix guide and FAQ.
Why Pool pH Keeps Rising
