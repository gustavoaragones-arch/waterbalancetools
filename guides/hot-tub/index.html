Featured Content

Hot Tub Alkalinity Too High: What to Do

Hot tub total alkalinity too high? Above 150 ppm causes cloudy water, scale, and pH that won't stay down. Step-by-step fix.
Hot Tub Chlorine Too High: What to Do

Hot tub chlorine too high? FC above 10 ppm causes irritation. Steps to lower it fast: remove cover, run jets, or dilute. Full guide.
Hot Tub Chlorine Too Low: Causes and Fix

Hot tub chlorine too low? Below 1 ppm, bacteria can grow in minutes at spa temperatures. Causes, fast fix, and prevention guide.
Hot Tub pH Too Low: Causes and Fix

Hot tub pH too low? Below 7.2 corrodes equipment, irritates skin, and destabilizes chlorine. Step-by-step fix with dose guide.
How Often to Shock a Hot Tub

How often to shock a hot tub: weekly minimum, after every 2–3 soaks, or when water looks dull. Shock schedule and product guide.
